2. What stays on your device

RFIDapp reads RFID and NFC tags through the iPhone's NFC interface — the tag's UID and other technical tag data. That data, along with the entries, group names, comments and scan history you create, is stored only on your device.

UIDs, comments, group names and free text never leave your device. They are not transmitted to me or to anyone else. A CSV or JSON export is produced on your device; where you share it is up to you.

3. NFC access

RFIDapp requests permission to access the iPhone's NFC interface when scanning. Without this permission the app cannot read tags.

4. Anonymous usage statistics

With your consent, the app collects anonymous usage statistics through Firebase Analytics. The sole purpose is to see which features are used and where scans fail, which is what sets the priority for the next improvements.

What is collected:

  • a pseudonymous app instance identifier that Firebase assigns to your installation — no name, no Apple ID, no advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • in-app events: which screen was opened, whether a scan was started, succeeded, was cancelled or failed, which tag family was detected (for example MIFARE/NTAG or ISO 15693), the length of the UID in bytes, how long a cancelled scan ran, and actions involving groups, export, the paywall and the survey;
  • technical context: app and build version, device model, iOS version and language setting;
  • an approximate location at country or region level, which Firebase derives from your IP address. The IP address itself is not retained in the reports.

Tag UIDs, comments, group names, history entries and free text are not collected. The statistics contain only types, reasons and counts.

You can turn usage statistics on and off at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy → Anonymous usage statistics. While the switch is off, nothing is collected and nothing is sent.

5. Feedback form

The app includes a short, optional survey. Only if you submit it are the following details sent to Cloud Firestore: your rating, the features you selected, your free-text answers about problems and feature requests, app and build version, language setting, platform, where you opened the survey, the survey version and the time of submission.

The email field is optional. If you enter an address, it is stored with your answers and used only to reply to your feedback. If you leave it empty, no address is sent.

6. Recipients and transfer to the USA

For usage statistics and feedback I use Firebase, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, which may also transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Google acts as a processor under a contract pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR. Transfers to the USA rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses; Google LLC is additionally certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Feedback data is held in the Firebase project rfid-app-b2119 in region eur3 (Europe: Belgium and the Netherlands). There are no other recipients: no data goes to advertising networks, data brokers or third-party analytics services, and there is no tracking across apps or websites.

7. Legal bases

Local processing on your device is part of the functionality you invoke by using the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Because this data never leaves the device, it is not processed by me.

Usage statistics and feedback rely on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and for storing the identifier on your device additionally § 25(1) TDDDG, the German implementation of the ePrivacy rules). Consent is voluntary and the app is fully functional without it. You can withdraw it at any time using the switch in Settings, at no disadvantage to you. Withdrawal takes effect going forward.

8. Data retention

Data stored locally — history, entries, groups, comments — stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Statistics data is deleted automatically after 2 months. Feedback responses are deleted once they have been reviewed and any reply has been sent, and after 24 months at the latest.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access the data held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent you have given. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — the one responsible for me is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg, Germany.

To access or delete statistics data I need the app instance identifier, because otherwise I cannot match a request to it: it is pseudonymous and not known to me by itself. If you submitted feedback with an email address, that address is enough.
